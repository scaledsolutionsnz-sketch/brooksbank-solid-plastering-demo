FAQ

Before you ring

Anything else, just ask David.

What areas do you cover?

Based in Greenhithe, working across the North Shore, Auckland City, West Auckland, the Hibiscus Coast and Rodney. Waiheke Island jobs too. If you're further out, give David a call and ask.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Quotes and advice are free. Call or email with the address and what needs plastering, and David will arrange a time to come and look.

Can you fix cracks in my existing plaster?

Yes, repairs are a big part of the work. Email a few photos of the cracks with your address so David can see what he's dealing with before he comes out.

Do you only do houses?

No. Brooksbank plasters houses, offices and warehouses, from small jobs like a set of steps up to full exteriors.

What should I have ready when I get in touch?

The site address, what you want plastered (walls, steps, a whole house, repairs), rough sizes if you know them, and a couple of photos. For timing and access questions, like whether your job needs scaffolding, ask David when he's on site.

What happens after I accept the quote?

You and David lock in a start date and go over access to the site. Ask him about the steps involved for your particular job so you know what to expect.

Contact

Got plaster that needs doing?

Call David for a free quote, or email the job details and a few photos. He's on the tools most days, so if he misses your call, leave a message.
