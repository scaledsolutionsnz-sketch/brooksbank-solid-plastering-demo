Privacy Policy

Brooksbank Solid Plastering respects your privacy and follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you for personal information. If you call or email, we receive the details you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email, site address and photos of the job.

How we use it

We only use your details to answer your enquiry, quote and carry out the work, and keep in touch about that job. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Third-party services

The email button opens Gmail, and the map is provided by Google Maps. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Call 021 265 0998.
